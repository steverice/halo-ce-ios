VISR

Experience Halo: Combat Evolved in a whole new dimension

VISR is a native port of Halo: Combat Evolved for Apple Vision Pro and other Apple devices: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. Based on a lot of hard work from the community, optimized for Metal and Apple Silicon.

VISR aims to provide the best possible way to experience Halo while staying faithful to the original game. VISR builds on my love of the game and the many thousands of hours I have spent playing it.

On every device

  • iCloud saves

    Your saves and player profiles sync across your devices through iCloud.

  • It just works

    VISR is tailored to each Apple device, so it just works.

  • Compatible with the community

    VISR aims for cross-play with the popular Halo: CE ports, such as OpenCE and Chupathingy.

Apple Vision Pro

  • Full stereo VR with head tracking and foveated rendering
  • Gameplay elements reimagined for a first-class VR experience
  • The HUD in CE's own corners, out in the periphery, following the body's turn rather than the head
  • Scope zoom fills the view
  • Third-person cutscenes on a large 3D screen in the room
  • Support for PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers
  • Supports both M2 and M5 Vision Pro

iPhone and iPad

  • Built-in touch controls for an on-the-go experience
  • Full controller support
  • Play on a connected TV or monitor
  • iOS 16+, iPhone 8 or newer

Apple TV

  • Full controller support
  • Split-screen multiplayer on one TV

Mac

  • Native AppKit app
  • Keyboard and mouse support
  • Any window, any size, full screen or not
