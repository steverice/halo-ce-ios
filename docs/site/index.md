# VISR

## Experience Halo: Combat Evolved in a whole new dimension

VISR is a native port of Halo: Combat Evolved to for Apple Vision Pro and other Apple devices: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac, Based on a lot of hard work from the community, optimized for Metal and Apple Silicon.

The goal is to provide the best possible ways to experience Halo while staying faithful to the original game. VISR builds on my love of the game and the many thousands of hours I have spent playing it.

## Apple Vision Pro

- Full stereo VR with head tracking and foveated rendering
- Gameplay elements reimagined for a first-class VR experience
- Full support for Playstation VR2 Sense controllers
- Supports both M2 and M5 Vision Pro

## iPhone and iPad

- Built in touch controls for an on-the-go experience
- Full controller support
